Hi hello this is my work-in-progress thingsmod! It's basically just a collection of features that I think improve the game experience that aren't large enough to warrant their own mod nor fit into any of  my other mods. I generally am seeking to fix what I think is broken about the game, fill in what's missing and overall increase quality of life and cohesion of features.

You can read up on the features of this mod in FEATURES.md
Currently the mod does not have mod settings, as i hate working on mod settings :(
I'll finish them before I release the mod, but in the meantime you can use config.lua to toggle specific features.
